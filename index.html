Banking that moves at the speed of your day.

We build Asenso Mobile — a mobile banking app that lets Filipino customers open an account, move money, pay bills and get paid, from a phone, without a trip to a branch.

Asenso Mobile is coming to Google Play and the App Store.

What you can do in Asenso Mobile

Everyday banking, built for how money actually moves in the Philippines.

Open an account from your phone

Guided digital onboarding with identity verification — no branch visit to get started.

Accounts and balances

See your accounts, balances and transaction history in one place, updated as you transact.

Fund transfers

Send money to your own accounts, to other customers, and to other banks and e-wallets.

Bills payment

Pay utilities, loans and other billers, and keep a record of every payment you make.

QR payments

Scan to pay merchants, or show your own QR to get paid.

Remittance and e-load

Send remittances and buy mobile load for any network, straight from your account.

Loans

View your loan accounts, balances and payment schedules.

Device and profile management

Register a new device, update your number, and manage the details on your profile.

Security

Asenso Mobile handles real money, so the app is built to banking standards from the ground up rather than hardened after the fact.

Encrypted in transit

Every connection between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Requests are signed so they cannot be tampered with in flight.

Credentials stay on the device

Session tokens and keys are held in the device's hardware-backed secure storage — never in plain app storage.

One account, one device

Your account is bound to a registered device. Moving to a new phone requires a verification step, so a stolen password alone is not enough.

Step-up verification

One-time passwords and a transaction PIN protect sign-in and every money-moving action.
